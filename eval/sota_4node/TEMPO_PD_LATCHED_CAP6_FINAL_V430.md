# TEMPO P/D latched controller: cap-six frozen result

## Final controller

The selected controller is
`tempo-pd-latched-bypass-rolling-credit6-401`:

- Keep a four-gap rolling median of measured arrivals.
- Latch decoder-local bypass for the session after observing a median gap of
  at most 39 ms.
- Enable backpressure only while the current median is at most 25 ms.
- Permit six decoder-local requests; route overflow through the real pinned
  `LMCacheConnectorV1` P/D path.

The monotone route latch and rolling credit remain separate. Cap six replaces
cap five because the stronger 8 ms / 100 ms-idle burst directly exercised both
backpressure settings: cap six capped one request and passed with worst paired
E2E +58.2 ms; cap five capped two and failed with worst paired E2E +435.5 ms.

## Evidence (allocation 57078464)

The frozen cap-six policy passed sparse-to-burst, stationary burst, stationary
steady, and the stronger burst. It used exact real-vLLM TP4 prefill to TP4
decode replicas and the official pinned LMCache connector for every remote
baseline/fallback request.

Reverse burst-to-steady was measured twice. The fixed-cap run won final-tail
E2E 5/8; a behaviorally equivalent run (all active decisions were cap six,
with identical routes and no capped request) won 7/8. Pooled reverse-tail E2E
is therefore 12/16 with a negative median; TPOT is 16/16. This variability is
reported rather than hidden behind a single successful run.

The final aggregate is generated by
`analyze_tempo_pd_latched_cap6_suite_v429.py`. It requires every primary
workload to pass, actual heavy-burst backpressure, at least 80% combined E2E
and TPOT wins, negative paired medians, non-regressing TPOT p90, no observed
E2E regression above 250 ms, and the explicit pooled reverse-tail gates.

## Scientific boundary

This is an actual-vLLM, actual-KV, same-allocation P/D system screen against
the pinned official LMCache remote-prefill route. It is stronger than the
earlier sidecar/component experiments. It is not yet an independent
multi-allocation confidence interval, a universal threshold calibration, or a
Mooncake result. The current environment has no audited runnable Mooncake
same-harness adapter; no proxy throughput number is used as a win claim.

The next integration step is to move these state transitions from the external
request router into vLLM's connector scheduler admission hook. That change must
preserve the same direct-local and remote-P/D semantics, request bytes, prompt
geometry, TP split, GPU budget, and cold-cache policy used here.
